Feel Better Faster With IV Hydration Therapy

When you're dehydrated, run-down, or recovering from illness, drinking water sometimes isn't enough — or isn't fast enough. Intravenous (IV) therapy delivers fluids, electrolytes, vitamins, and medications directly into your bloodstream for near-instant absorption and rapid relief.

At Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic, IV therapy is administered by licensed medical professionals in a safe, comfortable clinical setting — not a spa. Every treatment begins with a medical evaluation to ensure IV therapy is safe and appropriate for you.

Our IV Therapy Services

IV Hydration for Dehydration

Hawaii's heat, sun, and active lifestyle make dehydration common — especially for visitors, athletes, and outdoor workers. IV hydration restores fluid balance quickly, relieving headaches, dizziness, fatigue, and nausea far faster than oral fluids alone.

Illness Recovery Drips

Fighting the flu, food poisoning, or a stubborn cold? IV fluids with electrolytes, anti-nausea medication, and vitamins help your body recover when you can't keep fluids down. Often paired with on-site lab testing to identify the underlying cause.

Vitamin & Wellness Infusions

Boost energy, immunity, and overall wellness with vitamin infusions including B-complex, vitamin C, and other nutrients — popular with travelers battling jet lag, busy professionals, and anyone feeling run-down.

Hangover & Jet Lag Relief

Visiting Waikiki and feeling the effects of travel or a long night out? A targeted IV drip rehydrates, replenishes, and gets you back to enjoying Hawaii — fast.

What to Expect During Your IV Visit

  1. Medical evaluation — a provider reviews your symptoms, history, and vital signs
  2. Personalized recommendation — the right fluids, electrolytes, vitamins, or medications for your needs
  3. Comfortable treatment — relax in our clinic while the IV infuses, typically 30–45 minutes
  4. Aftercare guidance — clear instructions and follow-up recommendations before you leave

Medical IV Therapy vs. Spa Drips

Unlike wellness spas, our IV therapy is provided within a full medical clinic. If your symptoms point to something more serious, our providers can diagnose and treat it on the spot — including urgent care for minor emergencies. Walk in daily 8:30 AM – 6:30 PM or call 808-470-5555.
