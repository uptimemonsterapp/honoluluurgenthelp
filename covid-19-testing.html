Fast, Reliable COVID-19 Testing — Walk In 7 Days a Week

Whether you have symptoms, were recently exposed, need clearance for travel or work, or just want peace of mind before visiting family, Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic makes COVID-19 testing simple. Walk in any day of the week — no appointment needed — and get tested by our professional clinical team with results you can trust.

As a full medical clinic, we go beyond the swab: if you test positive or your symptoms suggest something else, our providers can evaluate and treat you on the spot — something pop-up testing sites simply can't offer.

COVID-19 Tests We Offer

Rapid Antigen Test

  • Results typically within 15–30 minutes
  • Best for: symptoms, recent exposure, quick screening
  • Same-visit documentation for work or school

PCR (Molecular) Test

  • The gold standard for accuracy
  • Best for: travel requirements, pre-surgical clearance, confirming rapid results
  • Official lab report accepted by airlines, employers & institutions

Who Should Get Tested?

  • Anyone with symptoms: fever, cough, sore throat, congestion, fatigue, loss of taste or smell
  • People with a known exposure to someone who tested positive
  • Travelers needing pre-departure or return testing documentation
  • Workers and students with employer or school testing requirements
  • Anyone visiting elderly or immunocompromised loved ones

Tested Positive? We've Got You Covered.

A positive test is just the beginning of our care. Our providers will evaluate your symptoms, discuss treatment options, provide isolation and recovery guidance, and — if needed — support your recovery with IV hydration therapy for dehydration or urgent care for worsening symptoms. We can also check for flu and other respiratory illnesses with our on-site laboratory testing, since symptoms often overlap.

Stay protected going forward — ask about COVID-19 boosters and flu shots through our immunizations & vaccinations program during your visit.

Need a Test for Travel?

Check your destination's requirements before your visit (test type and timing windows vary). Bring your travel details and we'll make sure you get the right test with the right documentation. Questions? Call 808-470-5555.

Frequently Asked Questions

Rapid antigen results are typically ready within 15–30 minutes, before you leave. PCR test results depend on lab processing times — ask our team at check-in for the current turnaround, and we'll deliver results as soon as they're available.

Requirements vary by destination, airline, and purpose. Most international destinations that require testing accept PCR; some accept supervised rapid antigen tests. Bring your travel requirements to your visit and we'll ensure you receive the correct test and official documentation.

No appointment is needed — walk in any day of the week, 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM. If you're experiencing symptoms, let our front desk know when you arrive so we can keep you and other patients comfortable and safe.
