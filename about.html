About Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic

Compassionate, Accessible, Same-Day Medical Care in the Heart of Honolulu

Home › About Us

Quality Care, When You Actually Need It

Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic was founded on a simple belief: getting great medical care shouldn't require weeks of waiting, complicated referrals, or hours in an emergency room. Located in the Ala Moana Building at 1441 Kapiolani Boulevard, our clinic serves residents, workers, students, and visitors across Honolulu County with fast, affordable, walk-in medical care — 7 days a week, 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Our team of board-certified providers combines the convenience of an urgent care with the continuity of a primary care practice through our Primary Care+ program. From urgent care & minor emergencies to health & wellness checkups, we treat the whole person — not just the symptom.

A Full-Service Clinic Under One Roof

Our modern facility offers far more than basic urgent care. Patients choose us for on-site laboratory testing, IV hydration therapy, orthopedic injury care, immunizations & travel vaccines, COVID-19 testing, and transitional care after hospital discharge — all in one convenient Honolulu location.
