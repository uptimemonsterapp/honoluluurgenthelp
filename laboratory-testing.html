On-Site Lab Testing — Answers Without the Runaround

Getting lab work shouldn't mean juggling separate lab appointments, waiting days for results, and then booking another doctor's visit just to hear what they mean. At Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic, our modern facility features an in-house laboratory for fast, accurate diagnostics — testing, results, and treatment all under one roof, often in a single visit.

Whether you need routine blood work, a rapid strep test, a pre-employment screening, or diagnostic testing for an illness, simply walk in. No appointment, no referral hassle, no long waits.

Lab Tests We Offer

Rapid & Point-of-Care Testing (Same-Visit Results)

  • Strep throat (rapid strep test)
  • Influenza (flu) testing
  • COVID-19 rapid antigen & PCR testing
  • Urinalysis & urinary tract infection (UTI) testing
  • Pregnancy testing
  • Mononucleosis (mono) testing
  • Blood glucose (sugar) testing

Blood Work & Diagnostic Panels

  • Complete blood count (CBC)
  • Comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP)
  • Lipid panel (cholesterol)
  • Thyroid function (TSH) testing
  • Hemoglobin A1C (diabetes screening)
  • Vitamin & nutrient deficiency panels
  • STD / STI screening panels

Employment, School & Travel Testing

We provide drug screenings, TB testing, titers (immunity verification for vaccinations), and other occupational health testing with the documentation employers and schools require.

Why Choose Our In-House Lab?

  • One-stop care — if your results show a problem, our providers treat you on the spot
  • Accuracy — modern diagnostic equipment and strict quality controls
  • Convenience — walk in any day, 8:30 AM – 6:30 PM, including weekends

Frequently Asked Questions

No appointment is needed — walk in any day of the week. If another provider ordered your labs, bring the order with you. If you're not sure what testing you need, our providers can evaluate you and order the right tests during your visit.

Rapid tests (strep, flu, COVID-19 antigen, urinalysis, pregnancy) deliver results during your visit — usually within 15–30 minutes. Blood panels and cultures typically take 1–3 business days, and we contact you promptly with results and next steps.

Some tests — like lipid panels and fasting glucose — require 8–12 hours of fasting. Others don't. If you're planning specific blood work, call us at 808-470-5555 beforehand and we'll tell you exactly how to prepare.
