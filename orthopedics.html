Expert Orthopedic Care Without the ER Wait

A twisted ankle on a Waikiki trail, a wrist injury from a weekend surf session, a swollen knee that appeared out of nowhere — musculoskeletal injuries can't wait weeks for a specialist appointment. At Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic, our providers evaluate and treat a wide range of orthopedic conditions the same day you walk in, at a fraction of emergency room cost and wait time.

From initial evaluation and stabilization to splinting, pain management, and specialist referrals when needed, we make sure your bones, joints, and muscles get the right care — right away.

Orthopedic Conditions We Treat

Sprains & Strains

Ankle sprains, wrist sprains, and muscle strains are among the most common injuries we see. We assess severity, rule out fractures, provide splinting or bracing, and give you a clear recovery plan.

Fractures & Suspected Broken Bones

For suspected fractures, we perform a thorough evaluation with X-ray referral, immobilize the injury with splints, manage your pain, and coordinate follow-up with orthopedic specialists for casting or advanced care.

Joint Pain & Arthritis Flare-Ups

Sudden joint swelling, gout attacks, and arthritis flare-ups can be debilitating. We provide fast relief, evaluate for infection or other causes with on-site laboratory testing, and help you manage flare-ups safely.

Sports & Work-Related Injuries

From tennis elbow and runner's knee to lifting injuries on the job, we treat active adults and workers — and provide the documentation employers need for work-injury claims. We also support your recovery through transitional care follow-ups after more serious injuries.

When to Go to the ER Instead

  • Bone visibly protruding through the skin (open fracture)
  • Severe deformity or a limb that looks misshapen
  • Numbness, blue color, or loss of circulation below the injury
  • Head, neck, or spine injury with neurological symptoms
  • Uncontrolled bleeding

For these emergencies, call 911 or go directly to the nearest emergency room. For everything else, our urgent care & minor emergencies team is the faster, more affordable choice.
