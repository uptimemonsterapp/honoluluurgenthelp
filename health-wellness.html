What Is Included in a Health and Wellness Checkup?

A health and wellness checkup is a proactive way to stay ahead of potential concerns. At Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic, we offer a comprehensive review focused on your overall well-being, not just treating symptoms. A visit generally includes:

  • Vital sign monitoring (blood pressure, heart rate, temperature)
  • Review of your health history
  • Screenings for diabetes, cholesterol, and blood pressure
  • Complete physical exam
  • Mental health check-in and stress assessment
  • Lifestyle discussions on sleep, physical activity, and nutrition
  • Vaccination review and updates
  • Lab tests, if needed
