When Life Happens, We're Here — No Appointment Necessary

A sudden fever at midnight planning, a kitchen cut that needs stitches, a child's earache on a Sunday — minor emergencies never check your schedule. At Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic, you don't need one either. Walk in any day of the week, 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM, and be seen by board-certified providers — most patients are treated and back to their day in under an hour.

We're the smart alternative to the emergency room for non-life-threatening conditions: significantly lower cost, dramatically shorter waits, and the same quality medical care — with an in-house lab for fast, accurate diagnostics.

  • Strep throat, ear infections & sinus infections

Diagnostics & Treatment On-Site

Our clinic features rapid strep, flu, and COVID-19 testing, full laboratory testing, IV hydration for dehydration, nebulizer treatments, wound repair, and more — diagnosis and treatment in a single visit.

Urgent Care vs. Emergency Room: How to Decide

Come to urgent care for: fevers, sprains, cuts needing stitches, minor burns, infections, flu symptoms, earaches, UTIs, and rashes.

Go to the ER or call 911 for: chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), head trauma, seizures, or any life-threatening emergency.
