What Is Transitional Care — and Why Does It Matter?

Leaving the hospital is a relief — but it's also one of the riskiest moments in a patient's healthcare journey. Studies consistently show that the first 30 days after discharge carry the highest risk of complications, medication errors, and hospital readmission. Transitional care bridges the gap between hospital and home, ensuring your recovery stays on track.

At Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic, our transitional care program gives you fast, walk-in access to follow-up care without waiting weeks for a primary care appointment. Whether you were treated for an infection, injury, surgery, or a chronic condition flare-up, our board-certified providers make sure nothing falls through the cracks.

Our Transitional Care Services in Honolulu

Post-Hospital Follow-Up Visits

We review your discharge summary, hospital records, and treatment plan — then check your progress, adjust your care plan, and coordinate with your hospital team or specialists when needed.

Medication Reconciliation & Management

Medication errors are the #1 cause of post-discharge complications. We carefully review every medication you're taking — new prescriptions, changed dosages, and medications you should stop — to keep you safe.

Recovery Monitoring & Symptom Checks

We monitor wound healing, infection signs, pain levels, mobility, and vital signs. If something isn't healing as expected, we act quickly — with on-site laboratory testing for fast answers.

Chronic Condition Support

After a hospitalization for heart failure, COPD, diabetes, or another chronic condition, we help you stabilize and build a sustainable management plan through our health & wellness and primary care+ services.

Who Benefits From Transitional Care?

  • Patients recently discharged from a hospital or emergency room
  • Seniors recovering from illness, falls, or surgery
  • Patients with multiple medications or complex care plans
  • Anyone whose primary care doctor can't see them quickly
  • Visitors to Hawaii who need follow-up care away from home
