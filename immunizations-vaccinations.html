Stay Protected With Convenient, Walk-In Vaccinations in Honolulu

Vaccines are one of the simplest, most effective ways to protect yourself, your family, and your community from serious illness. At Honolulu Urgent Care Clinic, getting vaccinated couldn't be easier — just walk in. No appointment, no long pharmacy lines, no waiting weeks for a doctor's visit. Our licensed medical team administers vaccines safely and quickly, so you're in and out in minutes.

Whether you need a routine flu shot, school-required immunizations, work-mandated vaccines, or travel protection before an international trip, we've got you covered — 7 days a week, 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Vaccines We Offer

Routine Adult & Childhood Immunizations

  • Influenza (flu shot) — seasonal protection for all ages
  • Tdap / Td (tetanus, diphtheria, pertussis)
  • Hepatitis A & Hepatitis B
  • MMR (measles, mumps, rubella)
  • Varicella (chickenpox)
  • Pneumococcal (pneumonia) vaccines
  • Shingles (Herpes Zoster) vaccine
  • HPV (human papillomavirus) vaccine

Travel Vaccines & Travel Medicine

Planning an international trip from Honolulu? Protect yourself before you fly. We offer travel health consultations and destination-specific vaccines, along with guidance on food, water, and mosquito-borne illness prevention. Pair your visit with a pre-travel wellness checkup for complete peace of mind.

School, Work & Immigration Vaccinations

We provide the documentation you need for school enrollment, employment requirements, healthcare worker clearance, and immigration medical needs — with records you can take with you the same day.

Why Get Vaccinated at Our Walk-In Clinic?

  • No appointment needed — walk in any day of the week
  • Fast visits — most vaccinations take under 20 minutes
  • Insurance accepted — most plans cover routine vaccines at no cost
  • Complete records — official documentation provided on the spot
  • Expert guidance — our providers review your vaccine history and recommend what you're missing

Not Sure Which Vaccines You Need?

Bring your immunization records (if available) and our team will review your history, check for gaps, and recommend the right vaccines for your age, health conditions, job, and travel plans. Call 808-470-5555 or contact us online.
